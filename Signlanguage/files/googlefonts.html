FRUITS

APPLE

Apples are one of the most widely consumed fruits globally, originating from the Malus domestica tree. They come in thousands of varieties, with different flavors ranging from sweet to tart. Popular varieties include Fuji, Gala, Granny Smith, and Red Delicious. Apples can be eaten raw, used in baking, or made into juices and ciders, offering versatility in the kitchen. Nutritionally, apples are a great source of dietary fiber, particularly in the skin, which aids digestion and promotes fullness. They are low in calories, making them a healthy snack option for those seeking to manage their weight. Additionally, apples provide essential vitamins, particularly vitamin C, which supports the immune system, as well as antioxidants that help protect the body from cellular damage caused by free radicals.

MANGO

The mango is a tropical fruit with hundreds of varieties that have been named "the king of fruits" due to its delicious flavor and many health benefits. The mango, scientifically known as Mangifera indica L., is luscious, sweet, and fragrant, and packed with nutrients like vitamins A and C.1 Mangoes also have the potential to offer health benefits such as heart and eye health support. What's more, mangoes are versatile as they can be used during breakfast, as a snack, and in desserts.

KIWI

Kiwi fruit is a powerhouse of nutrients, offering numerous health benefits. It's rich in vitamin C, which boosts the immune system and helps in collagen production for healthy skin. The fruit is also packed with antioxidants like flavonoids and carotenoids, which help protect cells from damage. Kiwi is a great source of dietary fiber, promoting healthy digestion and reducing constipation. Additionally, it contains potassium, which helps regulate blood pressure and supports heart health.

VEGETABLES

POTATO

Potatoes are a versatile and nutritious vegetable with several health benefits. They are an excellent source of carbohydrates, providing energy, and are rich in fiber, which supports digestive health. Potatoes also contain vitamin C, which boosts immunity and promotes skin health. High in potassium, they help regulate blood pressure and maintain heart health. Additionally, potatoes are packed with antioxidants, which can help reduce inflammation and protect against cell damage.

CARROT

Carrots are root vegetables known for their vibrant orange color, although they can also come in other colors like purple, yellow, red, and white. They belong to the Daucus carota species and are commonly consumed both raw and cooked. Carrots are a staple in many cuisines worldwide and are used in salads, soups, stews, and juices. Their slightly sweet flavor and crunchy texture make them a popular choice for both adults and children. Nutritionally, carrots are an excellent source of vitamin A, primarily from beta-carotene, which gives them their orange color. Vitamin A is crucial for eye health, supporting good vision and reducing the risk of age-related macular degeneration.

LADY FINGER

Lady finger, also known as okra, is a highly nutritious vegetable with numerous health benefits. It is rich in dietary fiber, which aids digestion and promotes a healthy gut. Okra is an excellent source of vitamin C, which helps boost the immune system and promotes healthy skin. It also contains antioxidants like flavonoids and polyphenols, which help protect against oxidative stress. Additionally, lady finger is a good source of folate, which supports cell growth and reduces the risk of birth defects. .
